Last updated: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DEXTA Browser Android application ("DEXTA", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

Important: DEXTA is a web browser. Websites you visit may independently collect information about you under their own privacy policies. This policy covers the DEXTA app itself, not those websites.

1. Information and browsing activity

When you use a browser, information such as visited website addresses, browsing history, bookmarks, downloads, cookies, and site data may be processed or stored so browser features can work. Whether and where this information is stored depends on the app version, your settings, and the features you use. Review the app's settings and controls to manage or clear locally stored browser data.

Do not assume that information entered into websites is handled by DEXTA. Forms, accounts, payments, and other content submitted to a website are generally handled by that website and its service providers.

2. Information we receive

The app may process information needed to provide features you choose to use, such as preferences, saved browser data, downloads, or update-check requests. This policy does not claim that DEXTA's developer receives your complete browsing history. Any optional feature that sends information to a server should explain what it sends and why before you use it.

3. Permissions

Android permissions may be requested for specific functions, such as downloading files or showing notifications about updates. You can review and change app permissions in Android Settings. The permissions available may vary by Android version and app version.

4. Third-party services and websites

DEXTA may connect to third-party websites or services when you browse, search, download files, or check for app updates. Those third parties operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control how external websites handle information you provide directly to them.

5. Data security and retention

Use your device's security features and keep Android and DEXTA updated. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Locally stored browser information may remain on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app; actual behavior can depend on the app version and Android backup settings.

6. Children's privacy

DEXTA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the app developer, contact us using the details below so the concern can be reviewed.
