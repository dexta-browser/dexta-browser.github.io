Privacy Policy

DEXTA Browser for Android · Last updated 28 November 2026

Your privacy matters to me. I don't collect your personal information: there is no account to create, and your history and passwords are never sent to me.

In short

  • No sign-up. Install DEXTA and start browsing.
  • No analytics or telemetry. I don't track how you use the app.
  • No selling of data. I have none of your data to sell.
  • Passwords stay on your phone, in an encrypted vault.
  • Protection built in. Ad and tracker blocking, third-party cookie blocking and Safe Browsing are on by default.
  • You are in control. You can turn off search suggestions, weather and news, and clear your browsing data in Settings.

Some features need to contact outside services to work. They are listed below so you know exactly what happens.

Data stored on your device

History, bookmarks, downloads, cookies, site data, settings, user scripts and saved passwords are kept on your device. Passwords are held in an encrypted vault. Backup and sync files are created only when you ask, and you choose where to keep them. You can clear browsing data in Settings or by uninstalling the app. Files you downloaded or exported may remain.

Services the app contacts

Some features send data directly from your device to third parties, which also receive your IP address and apply their own privacy policies:

  • Websites you visit, including requests for their icons.
  • Search engine: your searches, and the words you type if search suggestions are on (on by default).
  • Weather (Open-Meteo): approximate coordinates or the city name you search for. On by default; can be turned off.
  • News (Google News, BBC or a feed you add): requests for headlines. On by default; can be turned off.
  • Google Translate: the text you translate, or pages you set to translate automatically.
  • Google Safe Browsing (through Android WebView): checks for dangerous sites. Can be turned off.
  • GitHub: update checks and update downloads. Automatic checks can be turned off.
  • Filter list providers (EasyList and others): ad-blocking list downloads.
  • Secure DNS (Cloudflare, Google or Quad9), only if you enable it.

Permissions

  • Location: local weather, and websites you allow.
  • Camera and microphone: only for websites you allow.
  • Notifications and background services: downloads, media playback and updates.
  • Biometrics: app and password lock. Android handles the check; DEXTA never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Install packages: installing APK files and updates that you approve.

Children

DEXTA does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
